Yehudis — the artist

“There are things a person carries that have no words. I paint for those things.”

Yehudis Jacobs was born and raised in Jerusalem, where light, stone, and prayer shaped her earliest sense of beauty. She studied fine art at the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design, where she developed her signature layered oil technique. Her canvases are built from bold brushstrokes, luminous pigment and real gold leaf.

She holds a master's degree in art therapy, a path rooted in her conviction that painting is a language of its own — one capable of holding grief, joy, longing, and faith in ways that ordinary words cannot reach. Her work is not decoration. It is a vessel.

Yehudis's paintings are held in private collections across Israel, the United States, and Europe. She accepts commissions for original works and is available for consultation on pieces created for the Jewish home.
