About Yehudis

I paint what
words cannot
reach.

Yehudis is a contemporary spiritual artist whose paintings explore the meeting point between memory, emotion, and inner light.

Her work is built through layers of color, texture, movement, and silence. Each painting carries a sense of presence — not only to be seen, but to be felt.

With a Master's degree in Art Therapy, Yehudis brings a deeper understanding of the emotional power of art.

Her work speaks to collectors, interiors, and spaces seeking depth, beauty, and quiet spiritual strength.
