About the artist

I have always seen art as the language of the soul.

Some experiences, emotions, and truths live beyond words. Art has the ability to reach those places, giving shape to what we often feel long before we can explain it.

My hope is that everyone who encounters my work experiences it in a deeply personal way. I don’t believe a painting should tell the viewer what to see. I believe it should create space for discovery. The same painting may speak differently to different people, and even to the same person at different moments in life. The longer we live with a work of art, the more it has the potential to reveal.

If my paintings inspire reflection, bring comfort, awaken wonder, or quietly invite someone to see life from a new perspective, then they have fulfilled their purpose.

Painting is my way of expressing what matters most to me and of sharing it with others.

As an Art Therapist with a Master’s degree, I have witnessed the extraordinary power of creativity, both in the lives of those I work with and in my own life. Again and again, I have seen that art offers us a path to express what words cannot, to discover hidden parts of ourselves, and sometimes, to begin healing.

One of the greatest gifts of the creative process is that it continues to surprise me. Every painting becomes a journey of discovery. Often, something unexpected emerges along the way, something I could never have planned from the beginning. Those moments remind me that true creativity is not only about making something beautiful. It is also about listening.

My artistic education includes studies at the Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design in Jerusalem, along with years of private study under internationally recognized artists. I am deeply grateful for everything they taught me. Yet the greatest teacher has always been life itself. Every encounter, every challenge, every moment of faith, joy, longing, and growth has become part of the way I paint.

Living in Jerusalem, I draw continual inspiration from the timeless beauty of Jewish life, Torah, and the spiritual richness woven into everyday moments.

My aspiration is to express the language of the spirit through the language of art, creating paintings that continue to reveal themselves over time and become meaningful companions in the homes and lives of those who welcome them.
